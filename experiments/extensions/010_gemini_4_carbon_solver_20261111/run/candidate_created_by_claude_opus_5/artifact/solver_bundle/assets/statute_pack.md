# MARNHOLT LEGISLATIVE SOURCE PACK

## Harbour Dues and Aquaculture Levy Act 2011 and all instruments affecting it

This pack is a complete set of primary and subordinate legislative materials for
the fictional Republic of Marnholt. It contains, in Part A, the interpretation
and application rules that govern how the materials in Parts B and C fit
together; in Part B, the Harbour Dues and Aquaculture Levy Act 2011 as
originally enacted; and in Part C, every instrument that has since amended,
supplemented, suspended or displaced it, in chronological order of making.

Nothing outside this pack is relevant. There is no case law. There is no
practice note. Every number, date and condition needed to answer a case is
either in this pack or in the facts of the case.

---

# PART A - INTERPRETATION AND APPLICATION RULES

**A1 (Completeness of this pack).** This pack reproduces every Act, order and
notice that affects the Harbour Dues and Aquaculture Levy Act 2011 ("the
principal Act"). No other instrument affects it. Where an Act confers power to
appoint a day on which a provision comes into operation, that power has been
exercised only to the extent shown by a commencement notice reproduced in Part
C. A provision for which no day has been appointed has never come into
operation and is not law.

**A2 (Textual amendments).** A textual amendment ("for X substitute Y",
"insert", "repeal") alters the text of the principal Act with effect from the
time the amending provision comes into operation. Where the amending instrument
contains an application provision, that provision determines the cases to which
the altered text applies.

**A3 (Default temporal rule).** Section 12 of the principal Act states the
default rule: a levy is computed by reference to the principal Act as in force
on the date of the landing. That default is displaced only so far as an
application provision expressly provides otherwise.

**A4 (Commencement and dates).** A provision that commences on a stated day
comes into operation at the start of that day. "On or after" a date includes
that date. "Before" a date excludes that date. A period expressed as running
from one date to another "(both dates inclusive)" includes both endpoints.

**A5 (Expiry of temporary provisions).** Where an Act ceases to have effect on
expiry, its provisions stop applying to events occurring after expiry. Expiry
does not alter the text of the principal Act and does not revive or repeal
anything.

**A6 (Revocation of subordinate instruments).** A revoked order has no effect in
relation to any landing occurring on or after the date from which the
revocation takes effect. An order that is revoked before the date from which it
was to have effect never affects any landing.

**A7 (No intermediate rounding).** Every amount is computed exactly, retaining
fractions of a mark. Rounding under section 11 of the principal Act is applied
once, to the final amount of each levy, and to nothing else. Percentages are
applied to the exact amounts specified in the provision that creates them.

**A8 (More than one levy).** Where a case asks for a total in respect of more
than one levy, each levy is computed and rounded separately under section 11
and the rounded amounts are then added. Where a single levy is charged in
respect of an aggregated landing, section 11 is applied once to that levy.

**A9 (Facts).** The facts stated in a case are true, complete and exhaustive.
Where the facts do not establish that a supplement, allowance, discount,
rebate, saving or exemption applies, it does not apply. Every landing described
in a case is a landing of farmed aquatic produce by a licensed operator at a
scheduled harbour, and the operator is liable for the levy.

**A10 (Currency and answers).** All amounts are in marks. Every levy figure
produced under this pack, after rounding under section 11, is a whole number of
marks.

# PART B - HARBOUR DUES AND AQUACULTURE LEVY ACT 2011

**Act 17 of 2011. Text as originally enacted.**

**1. Short title and commencement.**
This Act may be cited as the Harbour Dues and Aquaculture Levy Act 2011 and
comes into operation on 1 January 2012.

**2. Application.**
This Act applies to landings at scheduled harbours in Marnholt.

**3. Interpretation.**
In this Act -

- "assessment" means a notice of assessment issued under section 10, and the
  "assessment date" is the date on which the notice is issued;
- "body corporate" means a company or other legal person incorporated under the
  law of Marnholt or of any other place; an individual trading on their own
  account is not a body corporate;
- "community quay" means a quay designated as a community quay by the harbour
  authority;
- "gross landed weight" means the total weight of produce discharged in a
  landing, expressed in tonnes;
- "landing" means the discharge of farmed aquatic produce from a vessel at a
  scheduled harbour, and a landing "occurs" on the date, and at the time, at
  which discharge commences;
- "operator" means the person in whose name produce is landed;
- "powered berth" means a berth at which shore electrical power is supplied to
  the vessel.

**4. Charge to levy.**
A landing levy is charged in respect of every landing. The operator is liable
to pay it.

**5. Computation of the levy.**
The levy in respect of a landing is the aggregate of -

- (a) the base amount under section 6, plus
- (b) the berth surcharge under section 8, less
- (c) the small operator allowance (if any) under section 9,

that aggregate being rounded in accordance with section 11.

**6. Base rate.**
The base amount is 40 marks for each whole tonne of chargeable tonnage.

**7. Chargeable tonnage.**
(1) The chargeable tonnage of a landing is the gross landed weight of the
landing, rounded up to the next whole tonne, less a free allowance of 2 tonnes.
(2) A gross landed weight that is already a whole number of tonnes is not
increased by the rounding up required by subsection (1).
(3) Chargeable tonnage cannot be less than nil.

**8. Berth surcharge.**
The berth surcharge is 150 marks where the vessel occupied a powered berth for
the landing, and 60 marks in any other case.

**9. Small operator allowance.**
(1) The small operator allowance is given where the operator was a small
operator in relation to the landing.
(2) The allowance is 20 per cent of the base amount.
(3) An operator is a small operator in relation to a landing if the operator's
total gross landed weight at all scheduled harbours in the calendar year
immediately preceding the calendar year in which the landing occurs did not
exceed 50 tonnes.

**10. Assessment.**
The harbour master must issue a notice of assessment in respect of every levy.
The date of issue of that notice is the assessment date.

**11. Rounding.**
The amount of a levy is rounded to the nearest multiple of 5 marks. Where the
amount is exactly midway between two multiples of 5 marks, it is rounded up.

**12. Law applicable to a landing.**
The levy in respect of a landing is computed by reference to this Act as in
force on the date of the landing, except so far as an application provision in
an instrument amending this Act provides otherwise.

**13. Minimum amount.**
The amount of a levy cannot be less than nil.

# PART C - INSTRUMENTS AFFECTING THE PRINCIPAL ACT

## C1. Aquaculture Levy (Amendment) Act 2014

**Act 3 of 2014.**

**1. Commencement.** This Act comes into operation on 1 July 2014.

**2.** In section 6 of the principal Act, for "40 marks" substitute "48 marks".

**3.** In section 9(2) of the principal Act, for "20 per cent" substitute
"25 per cent".

**4. Application.** The amendment made by section 2 has effect in relation to
landings occurring on or after 1 July 2014. The amendment made by section 3 has
effect in relation to landings occurring on or after 1 January 2015.

## C2. Harbour (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2016

**Act 9 of 2016.**

**1. Commencement.** Sections 2, 3 and 4 come into operation on such day or days
as the Minister may appoint by notice.

**2.** In section 7(1) of the principal Act, for "2 tonnes" substitute
"1 tonne".

**3.** After section 8 of the principal Act insert -

> **8A. Night landing supplement.**
> A night landing supplement of 90 marks is charged in respect of a landing that
> commences at or after 22:00 or before 05:00.

and in section 5 of the principal Act, after paragraph (b) insert -

> - (ba) plus the night landing supplement (if any) under section 8A,

**4.** After section 8A of the principal Act insert -

> **8B. Cold-chain rebate.**
> A cold-chain rebate of 200 marks is given in respect of a landing where the
> produce is held at a temperature below 4 degrees Celsius at the moment of
> discharge.

and in section 5 of the principal Act, after paragraph (ba) insert -

> - (bb) less the cold-chain rebate (if any) under section 8B,

**5. Application.** An amendment made by a section of this Act has effect in
relation to landings occurring on or after the day appointed for the coming
into operation of that section.

## C3. Harbour (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2016 (Commencement) Notice 2016

**S.I. 2016/14. Made 6 September 2016.**

The Minister, in exercise of the power conferred by section 1 of the Harbour
(Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2016, appoints the 1st day of October 2016 as
the day on which sections 2 and 3 of that Act come into operation.

## C4. Aquaculture Levy (Amendment) Act 2018

**Act 21 of 2018.**

**1. Commencement.** This Act comes into operation on 1 January 2019.

**2. Repeal of small operator allowance.** Section 9 of the principal Act is
repealed, and in section 5 of the principal Act paragraph (c) is repealed.

**3. Savings.** Despite section 2, section 9 and section 5(c) of the principal
Act continue to have effect, as they had effect immediately before 1 January
2019, in relation to a landing that -

- (a) is made by an operator who held a Class C licence on 31 December 2018, and
- (b) occurs before 1 January 2022.

**4. Community quay discount.** After section 9 of the principal Act insert -

> **9A. Community quay discount.**
> (1) The community quay discount is given where the landing takes place at a
> community quay.
> (2) The discount is 15 per cent of the aggregate of the base amount and the
> berth surcharge.

and at the end of the list of paragraphs in section 5 of the principal Act
insert -

> - (d) less the community quay discount (if any) under section 9A,

**5. Power to set the base rate.** In section 6 of the principal Act, the
existing text becomes subsection (1), and after it insert -

> (2) The Minister may by order substitute a different amount for the amount for
> the time being specified in subsection (1). An order under this subsection has
> effect in relation to landings occurring on or after the date specified in the
> order, which must not be earlier than the date on which the order is made.

**6. Application.** The amendments made by sections 2, 4 and 5 have effect in
relation to landings occurring on or after 1 January 2019.

## C6. Harbour Emergency Measures Act 2021

**Act 4 of 2021.**

**1. Commencement.** This Act comes into operation on 1 March 2021.

**2. Suspension of berth surcharge.** No berth surcharge under section 8 of the
principal Act is charged in respect of a landing occurring during the emergency
period.

**3. Emergency period.** The emergency period is the period from 1 March 2021 to
28 February 2022 (both dates inclusive).

**4. Expiry.** This Act ceases to have effect at the end of 28 February 2022.

## C11. Aquaculture Levy (Anti-Avoidance) Act 2024

**Act 2 of 2024.**

**1. Commencement.** This Act comes into operation on 1 January 2024.

**2. Aggregation of landings.** After section 7 of the principal Act insert -

> **7A. Aggregation of landings within 24 hours.**
> (1) Where two or more landings by the same operator at the same scheduled
> harbour occur within any period of 24 hours, those landings are treated as a
> single landing (an "aggregated landing") for the purposes of sections 6 and 7.
> (2) The gross landed weight of the aggregated landing is the sum of the gross
> landed weights of the constituent landings, and the free allowance under
> section 7(1) is given once only.
> (3) The berth surcharge under section 8 is charged in respect of each
> constituent landing, and the night landing supplement under section 8A is
> charged in respect of each constituent landing to which it applies.
> (4) The aggregated landing is treated as occurring on the date, and at the
> time, of the earliest constituent landing.
> (5) A single levy is charged in respect of the aggregated landing, and section
> 11 is applied once to that levy.

**3. Community quay discount restricted.** In section 9A(1) of the principal
Act, after "community quay" insert "and the operator is not a body corporate".

**4. Application.** The amendments made by this Act have effect in relation to
landings occurring on or after 1 January 2024.
